01 / The opportunityLooking ahead to 2027

More than events.
An ecosystem for ideas.

Civic Tech DC has spent years bringing people together around public-interest technology. I see an opportunity to build on that foundation by making it easier for a good idea to become a project, for a newcomer to become a contributor, and for lessons learned in one room to reach people far beyond it.

This is my personal working vision, not an approved organizational roadmap. These directions are invitations to explore possibilities with the community, not commitments to launch or fund particular programs.

01 / Knowledge

Make knowledge public

Turn community experience into resources anyone can adapt.

  • Grow the Civic Tech DC Playbook into a toolkit
  • Explore a DC civic innovation ecosystem map
  • Publish practical project guides and case studies
02 / People

Create pathways to participate

Make civic technology more approachable, from first workshop to sustained contribution.

  • Hands-on learning and workshops
  • Contributor and leadership pathways
  • Explore workforce development partnerships
03 / Experimentation

Give ideas room to grow

Make room to investigate civic problems and test early approaches.

  • Explore Civic Innovation Labs
  • Consider an incubator track
  • Build connections to local collaborators
02 / How it connectsLearning out loud

Gather. Experiment.
Share. Repeat.

I don't want us to create resources in isolation. Events, projects, and future Labs can generate practical experience; the Playbook can preserve what we learn so the next group starts further ahead.

01 — Connect

Bring people together

Events and collaborations surface problems, perspectives, and potential partners.

02 — Test

Build and learn

Projects and proposed Labs offer spaces to prototype and improve ideas.

03 — Share

Open up what we learn

Publish templates, lessons, case studies, and what didn't work.
